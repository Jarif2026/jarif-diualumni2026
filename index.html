Frequently Asked Questions

How do I register for the DIU Alumni Network?
Fill out the registration form at the bottom of this page with your student ID and current organization details. Our verification team will process it within 24 hours.
What is Jarif's Classroom mentorship program?
Founded by Md. Jafrul Islam Jarif, Jarif's Classroom offers specialized guidance in web software engineering, coding best practices, and tech career planning.
Is alumni membership free of cost?
Yes! Alumni membership, directory access, and standard networking events are completely free for all verified graduates of Daffodil International University.
